# Introduction to CompuTec AI

CompuTec offers two ways to use AI with SAP Business One. **CompuTec AI** is a virtual assistant that CompuTec is developing in stages. It is planned for SAP Business One and CompuTec ProcessForce, and it is not yet released. **CompuTec MCP Server** is available now. It lets AI assistants such as Claude answer questions from your live SAP Business One data.

This section contains the documentation for CompuTec MCP Server. When CompuTec AI is released, you will find its documentation here as well.

## CompuTec AI

**CompuTec AI** is planned as a virtual assistant that will guide your team step by step, carry out business tasks, build the reports you ask for, and help configure your system. CompuTec releases its capabilities in stages. The first capability in development is answering questions about **SAP Business One** and **CompuTec ProcessForce**.

You will decide how much control your team keeps. **CompuTec AI** is planned to work in three modes:

1. You approve every step.
2. You set the boundaries and **CompuTec AI** checks in only when a decision is needed.
3. **CompuTec AI** completes routine, low-risk tasks on its own.

**CompuTec AI** will respect your **SAP Business One** roles and permissions.

:::info[Note]

**CompuTec AI** is in development. The capabilities described here are planned, and this documentation does not yet cover them. To follow its progress or to register your interest, see the [**CompuTec AI page**](https://www.computec.one/solutions/computec/ai) on the CompuTec website.

:::

## CompuTec MCP Server

**CompuTec MCP Server** is part of **CompuTec AppEngine**, and you can use it today. With it, your team can:

- Ask questions about SAP Business One data in plain language, for example "Which Production Orders are late this week?"
- Combine data from several areas in one question
- Create documents and update master data, if the user has the required authorization

Users sign in with their own SAP Business One credentials and see only the data that their authorizations allow. For a full description, see [**Introduction to CompuTec MCP Server**](/docs/ai/mcp-server-for-sap-b1/mcp-server-for-sap-business-one).
